EZAide Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version: everything you put into EZAide stays on your iPad. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we don't share or sell it. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Who we are

EZAide is an iPad app that helps families and caregivers track routine care tasks, medications, feeds and supplies. "We" and "us" mean the developer of EZAide.

What the app stores, and where

To work, EZAide saves the information you and your caregivers enter, including:

  • Tasks, medications and feeds, with their names, times, frequencies and instructions
  • The names of the people you add and the 4-digit codes they use to sign in, plus the admin code
  • Supply and inventory counts
  • Emergency information you choose to enter
  • A change log of who checked off or changed which task, and when
  • App settings, such as theme and Day and Night hours

All of this is stored only on your iPad. It is never sent to us or to any server we run. If your iPad's own iCloud backup is turned on, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. That backup is managed by Apple under your Apple account, and we have no access to it.

What we collect

Nothing. EZAide does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party data tools, and it does not connect to the internet to send your information anywhere.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment, and we never receive your card details or other payment information. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active, so the app can unlock. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, EZAide schedules reminders on the iPad itself. They are created and delivered by the device, and no reminder content is sent to us.

Backups you create

EZAide lets you export a backup file and restore from it. You decide where that file goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive, email or AirDrop. Once it leaves the app, it is handled by whichever service you choose.

Backup files are not encrypted or password-protected. They contain everything in the app, including people's names, sign-in codes and care details. Store them somewhere private and only share them with people you trust.

Children's information

EZAide is meant for adults caring for someone, who is often a child. Caregivers may enter details about the person they care for. That information stays on the iPad, as described above, and we do not collect it. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your information

Because your information lives only on your iPad, you are in control of it. You can delete tasks or people in the app, use Start fresh in Admin settings to erase everything, or delete the app to remove all of its data from the iPad. Backup files you exported must be deleted separately wherever you saved them. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If we change how EZAide handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If an update ever starts sending information off the iPad, such as syncing between devices, we will explain it in the app and ask for your agreement first.
